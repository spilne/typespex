# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

TypeSpex contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, review technical
claims on their merits, accept constructive feedback, and make participation welcoming regardless
of experience, identity, background, or ability.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted
personal attention, publishing another person's private information, deliberate disruption, and
personal attacks presented as technical review.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces
where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private report form](https://github.com/spilne/typespex/security/advisories/new) with a title that
starts with `[Conduct]`. Do not include sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. If the private
form is unavailable, open an issue containing only a request for private maintainer contact.

Maintainers will review reports in confidence, gather necessary context, avoid conflicts of
interest, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a private warning, removal of
content, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against a reporter
or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

Knowingly false reports and misuse of the reporting process may themselves result in corrective
action. Good-faith reports are protected even when an investigation does not confirm a violation.
